Tip 01
Log every deadline the day you get the contract.
Do not rely on memory. Inspection period, EMD, appraisal, financing contingency, closing date -- log them all immediately, with alerts 48 hours before each one. One missed deadline can kill a deal.

Tip 02
Introduce all parties on day one.
The moment a file opens, send one email to everyone -- buyer, seller, escrow, lender, co-op agent. Set expectations, share contacts, confirm dates. It eliminates a week of confusion before things even start moving.

Tip 03
Follow up with the lender early -- not the week before closing.
Most delays happen because nobody nudged the lender until it was too late. Check in weekly once the loan is in process. Ask for a clear-to-close target by the appraisal deadline. Early pressure means fewer fire drills at the end.

Tip 04
Confirm closing day details 48 hours out -- with everyone.
Do not assume closing will happen just because it is on the calendar. Two days before: confirm time and location with escrow, verify funds are wired, make sure the final walkthrough is done. Closing day surprises are almost always avoidable.

Tip 05
Document everything -- even when a deal falls through.
Every email, amendment, and update should be logged and stored. A clean file protects you if a dispute comes up later. When you are running multiple deals at once, organized records are the only thing keeping you from dropping the ball.
